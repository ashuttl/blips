# blips

## Commit messages and release notes

Same rule as linecast, and it applies here even though this repo has a
game in it. Andrew writes these himself, or edits them before they
ship. When committing on his behalf:

- Subject line: `Area: what changed`, stated plainly. E.B. White is
  the model: direct and spare. The poetry is in what goes unsaid.
- Much of the existing history is lyrical; that was Claude, not
  Andrew's taste. Do not match it.
- Leave commits unpushed unless asked, so messages can still be
  reworded with `git commit --amend`.
